# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Malayalam-reading news audiences who come to MediaOne for daily coverage, then step into `/shelf` deliberately — not scanning, but settling in to read. Shelf readers are looking for analysis, interviews, columns, and long-form life stories; their context is closer to a magazine reader than a news skimmer: unhurried, expecting depth, comfortable spending several minutes on one piece.

## Product Purpose

`/shelf` is MediaOne's in-depth magazine section, separate from the fast-moving news desk. It exists to showcase editorial craft — Analysis, Interview, Column, Art and Literature, Life Story, Podcast, Videos — content that rewards a slower, more considered layout than the breaking-news grid. Success looks like a section that reads as a distinct, editor-curated magazine within the larger news site, not another feed of cards.

## Brand Personality

Editorial, confident, unhurried. Sits closer to a print magazine's news desk than a digital content farm: bylines matter, headlines are treated as headlines, and the page trusts long stretches of whitespace to let stories breathe.

## Anti-references

Not a card-grid content farm (identical thumbnail + headline + excerpt tiles repeated to infinity). Not a generic blog template. Not the noisy, ad-dense, cramped layout typical of regional news sites — `/shelf` should feel like the one calm, well-edited section of the site.

## Design Principles

- Structure like a section front, not a feed — a news editor's hierarchy of lead story, secondary stories, and a rail of the rest, not a uniform grid.
- Spacious but filled: generous whitespace and breathing room, but never empty — every dense whitespace choice should still feel intentional and layered with content (categories, bylines, related reads), not sparse for its own sake.
- Category identity: Analysis, Interview, Column, Art & Literature, Life Story, Podcast, Videos should each read as a distinct department, not interchangeable tags.
- Serif-led editorial typography for headlines and body, sans for structural metadata (byline, date, category) — consistent with the site's existing Wired-inspired DESIGN.md system.
- Malayalam-first: layout and type rhythm must hold up for Malayalam script at long line lengths, not just Latin placeholder text.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA contrast and touch-target sizing. No specific additional requirements known.
